## Why M9 is separate

Functional requirements (M3-Mx) describe **what** the system does.
Non-functional requirements (M9) describe **how well** it does them.

Both are needed for a complete SRS. Mixing them produces FRs like "system shall be fast" — neither testable as FR nor proper NFR.
